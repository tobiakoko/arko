The old site was a generic creative-agency site, so Arko Media Labs itself was "alternative (d): nobody knew what we do."

## Brand Script (StoryBrand)
- **Character:** Pastor Marcus — DFW suburban pastor, 100–500 member church. Wants first-time guests to find the church online and walk in the door. Aspirational identity: "the church everyone in [city] can find."
- **External problem:** guests search "church near me [city]" and the church doesn't show up; site is years old, service times wrong, broken on phones.
- **Internal problem:** feels invisible and embarrassed — the ministry is real but the door is hidden; volunteers are burned out maintaining the site.
- **Philosophical problem:** a church with open doors shouldn't need a marketing department to be found.
- **Villain:** the outdated template website + invisibility on Google.
- **Guide:** "I've seen great churches that nobody can find online." Authority: two churches already served (DLBC Tampa, DCLM Lewisville), IAAP CPACC-certified accessibility, one senior person who answers the phone, 90-day visibility guarantee. (No invented numbers — Rule 8.)
- **Process Plan:** ① Take the free 5-min Church Visibility Scorecard → ② 15-min call + custom plan → ③ We build it and guests start finding you.
- **Agreement Plan:** you own your site and domain from day one · month-to-month after the initial term · 90-day visibility guarantee (next month free) · plain-English monthly reports.
- **Call to Action:** Direct = "Book a 15-min call." Transitional = "Get my free Church Visibility Scorecard" (primary goal, Phase 1).
- **Failure:** another year of guests never finding you; volunteers maintaining a site nobody visits; the community never meeting them.
- **Success:** guests say "found you on Google" · service times always current · the church everyone in Frisco can find · pastor back to shepherding. Status: "the church everyone in [city] can find." Completeness: a website that works. Self-realization: pastor free to shepherd.
