# Positioning & Messaging

## Competitive Alternatives
- REACHRIGHT / Ministry Designs / Clover / The Church Co — template-farm monthly sites ($57–$199/mo), vendor-hosted, lock-in; REACHRIGHT's site does not leave with the client.
- Subsplash / Tithe.ly / Pushpay — ecosystem lock-in (app + giving + web).
- Church Digital — remote-only custom builder, no local presence.
- DFW generalists (LIFT, JSL, E67, Elements Group) — full-service agencies, $10K+ pricing, not church-specialist.
- DIY (Wix/Squarespace/Tithe.ly builder) — "free" but no design craft, no sermons/giving integration, no grant, no one accountable.

## Best-Fit Customer
Pastor David — established suburban church (150–800) or church plant, DFW or nationwide; non-technical; referral-driven; wants one person to "just handle it." See docs/CUSTOMER.md.

## Market Category
Existing category ("church website design") entered with a **subcategory** position: *custom + local + ownership + Ad Grant* — vs the template-farm and lock-in incumbents. No new category; clear differentiation inside the existing one.

## One-Liner
**"We help churches get found on Google — with a website they own, and the $10,000/month in free Google Ads most churches never claim."**

## Brand Script (StoryBrand)
- **Character:** Pastor David — wants first-time guests to find his church online, feel welcomed before they arrive, and walk in on Sunday.
- **Problem — external:** 2012-era website (wrong service times, not mobile, invisible on Google), no sermons/giving online, volunteer webmaster gone.
- **Problem — internal:** Embarrassed ("when guests Google us, I cringe"), overwhelmed ("I'm a pastor, not a webmaster"), guilty ("I know it matters and haven't fixed it"), distrustful of agencies.
- **Problem — philosophical:** "A church's message shouldn't be hidden behind an outdated website. Every person searching for hope deserves to find the church waiting for them."
- **Villain:** the outdated website — and the $10,000/month Google grant sitting unclaimed.
- **Guide:** Arko Media Labs. Empathy: "You're a pastor, not a webmaster — you shouldn't have to become one." Authority: one senior designer-engineer, live client sites, accessibility built in, local DFW.
- **Plan (process):** ① Get your free Church Website Health Check → ② Custom design + build (4–6 weeks) → ③ We keep it fast, fresh, and found — grant included.
- **Plan (agreement):** You own your site and domain from day one. No lock-in. Cancel anytime after launch. Plain-English monthly reports. One human, one-business-day response.
- **Call to action (direct):** "Get your free Church Website Health Check." **(transitional):** "See churches we've built" / "Read the Ad Grant guide."
- **Failure:** Guests click away in seven seconds; another family picks the church that showed up when they searched; the $10K/month stays unclaimed; another year of apologizing for the website.
- **Success:** A neighbor searches "church near me" Saturday night → your church first → service times, last week's sermon, "what to expect" → Sunday they walk in and say "I found you online." Sermons on every podcast app. Giving in one tap. The website does what the church does: welcomes people.
