## Lead Capture — the Health Check funnel (Phase 9)

**Funnel name (locked):** Church Website Health Check — one term everywhere.

### Landing page (`/health-check`) — hook: category + moving-toward
- H1: "What's your church's Digital Health Score?" — sub: "Answer 10 quick questions to see exactly where your church stands on Google — and get a personalized fix-it list."
- 3 Cs: Clarity (measures website, Google presence, the $10K grant) · Credibility (research-built + client logos) · Connection (you-language).
- Bonus: personalized PDF report with "3 easy wins you can do this week."
- CTA: "Start the Health Check (takes less than 3 minutes)"
- Ethical line: delivers real value, never baits into a disguised pitch.

### Questionnaire — 10 questions, email captured first, 3 scored categories + 2 qualifiers
Progress "Question 5 of 10" → "Almost done!" · 1–5 scale · max 50 pts.

| Category | Questions | Max |
|---|---|---|
| Findability | 1. "church near me" — how often on page 1? 2. Google Business Profile set up + updated? 3. Sermon/podcast content findable on Google? | 15 |
| First impression | 4. Service times/address/directions in 5 seconds on homepage? 5. How does the site look on a phone? 6. Last meaningful update? | 15 |
| The grant | 7. Heard of the Google Ad Grant? 8. If on it, is grant traffic reaching your site? | 10 |
| Qualifiers | 9. Priority: new families / members / fundraising / events 10. Size + timing (exploring / this month / this quarter / before Easter) | — |

### Results — honest score, 3 tiers, unique copy + CTA per tier
Category breakdown (Findability /15 · First impression /15 · Grant /10). Score never inflated (ethical rule).

| Tier | Score | Tone | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Hidden Church | 0–19 | "You're doing the work — Google just can't see it yet." First steps: GBP, service times above the fold | Free 20-min first-steps call |
| Found, but overlooked | 20–34 | "Neighbors find you — then leave." First-impression fixes + grant | Free Health Check report call |
| Sunday-ready | 35–50 | "Strong foundation — the $10K is the unlock." | Free grant strategy call |

PDF report fires instantly (personalized cover: name + church, scores, recommendations, 3 wins this week).

### Follow-up — segmented by tier, no spam
- Instant: results email + PDF · Abandon recovery: "Your progress is saved" (email-first makes it work)
- Tier 1 → foundational wins (3 emails) → first-steps call · Tier 2 → first-impression + grant → report call · Tier 3 → grant strategy ("leaving $10K/month on the table") → grant call
