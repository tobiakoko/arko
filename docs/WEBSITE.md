## Scorecard spec (Phase 9 — scorecard-marketing)

**Name:** DFW Church Visibility Scorecard. **Hook (category + moving-toward):** "How visible is your church on Google?" + promise: "Answer 10 questions in under 3 minutes — get your visibility score and a personalized fix-it list." **3 Cs:** clarity (score + fix list), credibility (2 churches served, CPACC), connection (pastor language: "Your church deserves to be found — help families discover it"). **Start CTA:** "Get my scorecard" (invariant label across the site — one action, one name; the 3-minute promise lives in the subcopy, not the button).

**Capture (before question 1, email-first):** name, church, email; phone optional. Abandoned leads retained via email-first + saved progress.

**Questionnaire — 13 questions, 10 scored across 5 categories (max 50), 3 unscored qualifying:**
- **Found on Google (2q):** ① search "church near me" in your city — in the map pack? (consistently 5 / sometimes 3 / no 1 / don't know 0) · ② Google Business Profile complete? (fully 5 / mostly 4 / partially 2 / not sure 1)
- **Website (2q):** ③ can a first-timer find service times + directions in under 30 seconds? (yes 5 / mostly 3 / no 1 / don't know 0) · ④ does the main action work on a phone? (yes 5 / somewhat 3 / no 1 / not sure 1)
- **Ownership (2q, higher weight):** ⑤ who owns your domain and site files? (we do 5 / our agency 2 / not sure 0) · ⑥ if you left your provider tomorrow, could you keep your site? (yes 5 / no 0 / not sure 1)
- **Maintenance (2q, higher weight):** ⑦ when was your site last updated? (≤30 days 5 / ≤6 months 3 / >1 year 1 / never 0 / not sure 1) · ⑧ who handles updates + security? (paid provider 5 / volunteer 2 / nobody 0 / not sure 1)
- **Accessibility (2q):** ⑨ does your site meet WCAG (screen-reader usable)? (yes 5 / not sure 1 / no 0) · ⑩ how confident are you a blind visitor could use your site? (confident 5 / somewhat 2 / not confident 0)
- **Qualifying (unscored, shapes the recommendation visibly):** ⑪ Sunday attendance (under 100 / 100–500 / 500+) · ⑫ your role (senior pastor / administrator / volunteer / other) · ⑬ budget for church website work this year? (yes / not yet / not sure)

Progress indicator ("Question 5 of 13", "Almost done!"). No salesy, leading, lookup, or jargon questions.

**Results — overall score /50 + category breakdown; tiers and CTAs:**
- **Low 0–24 (0–49%):** "This area needs attention." + 3 easy first steps (free) + strongest CTA: "Book a 15-min call — we'll fix the worst one with you."
- **Medium 25–38 (50–74%):** *the conversion sweet spot* — "Strong foundations with room to improve." + prioritized fix list + "Book a 15-min call — get a custom plan."
- **High 39–50 (75–100%):** "Excellent foundation — now keep it." + maintain offer (accessibility monitoring, care plan) + consult.
- One prioritized fix per category, per tier. **Never inflate/deflate the computed score** (Rule 8).
- **PDF report** (personalized cover, name, category analysis, recommendations) — extends into sales calls. Results email fired immediately; abandon-recovery email for incomplete starts.

**Funnel integration:** visit → 5s value → scorecard (email first) → tiered results → 15-min call → starter build → care plan.

## Conversion Elements (Phase 8 — cro-methodology)

### Objection/Counter table (placements at friction points)

| Objection | Counter | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust:** "Agencies lock me in — I lose my site when I leave" (Hibu/Townsquare pattern) | Wedge panel: "You own your site and domain from day one. Month-to-month after the initial term. If you leave, your site leaves with you." + MSA clause | Hero-adjacent (home) + pricing |
| **Trust 2:** "Who is this?" | 2 named churches served · CPACC · 90-day guarantee | Proof bar, hero |
| **Price:** "They quote me $5K+ and I don't know what I get" | Published "starting at": builds $1,500–3,000, care $97–200/mo | Pricing page + "starting at" on every card |
| **Price (CO-only):** "Am I overpaying?" | "Care plans from $97/mo — about $3 a day." (counter without stating objection) | Care-plans page |
| **Fit:** "Will it work for MY small church?" | Custom, not template; "churches in Frisco like yours" city pages; case studies (pending evidence) | City pages + case studies |
| **Timing:** "The committee will get to it someday" | Cost-of-delay stakes: "Right now, families are searching 'church near me' — when they can't find you, they find someone else." + seasonal urgency "before the next Easter rush" (genuine, not fake scarcity) | Hero stakes + contact |
| **Effort:** "We're volunteers — nobody maintains websites" | "Done for you": we build it, we update it ($97–200/mo), plain-English monthly report, one person answers the phone; 3-step plan | Plan section + care-plans |
| **Effort (CO-only):** "We could DIY on Wix" | "A site nobody maintains is invisible — your care plan keeps it current; volunteers keep ministry, not websites" | Care-plans FAQ |
