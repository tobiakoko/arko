# Customer

## Segments & Best-Fit Customer
| Segment | Fit | Why | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Established suburban churches (150–800)** | Best-fit | Real budget ($2.5–7K builds), care-plan + Ad Grant upsell potential, referral-rich | 1 |
| **Church plants (DFW)** | Strong | Fast decisions, brand+website kits, high future referral value | 2 |
| **DFW African diaspora churches** | Strong (launch wedge) | Inside DCLM/DLBC network, near-zero web presence, pastor-driven | 1 (first 3–5 clients) |
| **Faith-based nonprofits / ministries** | Good | Share the Ad Grant wedge, mission framing, slower committees | 3 |
| **Megachurches (1000+)** | Later | Custom $5–10K+, multi-campus, but long sales cycles and incumbent vendors | 4 |
| Home-service businesses | Deferred | Paused direction — revisit only if church path stalls (kill criteria) | — |
| Med spa | Deferred | Reserve only; compliance burden + no personal fulfillment | — |
