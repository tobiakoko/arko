# Customer

## Segments & Best-Fit Customer

**Best-fit customer (primary):** DFW suburban church, 100–500 members, in Frisco / McKinney / Lewisville / Garland / Mesquite; pastor who cares about guest experience; existing website older than 3 years or template-built; serves a growing community. Referral-fed through denominational networks. Willing and able: builds $1,500–3,000, care plans $97–200/mo.

**Secondary segment (expansion):** DFW home-service businesses — auto repair & collision first (Ron's Body Shop, ASA-NTX), then contractors/HVAC/plumbing. Higher economics (builds $2,500–7,500, retainers $800–2,500/mo). Same offer mechanics, separate landing path, higher PVP (auto 23/30, home-service 22/30).

**Tertiary segment (year-2 reserve):** DFW med spas — premium budgets ($2,500–4,000/mo), no outbound until church + home-service proof exists; compliance prep required (TMB 22 TAC §165.1, photo consent) before activation.
